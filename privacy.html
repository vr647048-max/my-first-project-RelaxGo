Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Information we collect

When you book a service, we may collect your name, mobile number, selected service, appointment date and time, and the GPS location you choose to share for provider navigation. Payment details for online payments are handled by Razorpay; TherapyOnWay does not ask you to send card, UPI PIN, CVV, password or OTP to us.

How we use information

We use booking information to confirm and provide your requested service, help the provider reach the appointment location, communicate about the booking, provide tracking/status updates, and handle payment or support requests.

Location information

GPS is requested only when you choose “Use my location” during booking. The location is used for service delivery and provider navigation. You can decline location permission, but a valid service location is required to complete an at-home booking.

Sharing

Booking information may be shared with the assigned service provider and relevant service/payment infrastructure only as needed to provide the service, process payment, prevent fraud, or comply with law. We do not sell customer information.

Payments

Online payments are processed through Razorpay and are subject to Razorpay's terms and privacy practices. Never share an OTP, UPI PIN or payment password with anyone claiming to be TherapyOnWay.

Data retention and security

We retain booking and transaction information for operational, accounting, dispute-resolution and legal requirements. We use access controls and server-side payment verification, but no internet service can promise absolute security.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about your booking information, request correction of inaccurate information, or raise a privacy concern, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

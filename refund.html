Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026

Customer cancellation

If you need to cancel or reschedule, contact TherapyOnWay as early as possible using the booking contact details. Requests made before the provider has started travelling to the appointment will be considered for cancellation and refund where applicable.

Online payments

For eligible online-payment cancellations, refunds are initiated through the applicable payment process and may take additional time to appear in the customer's account depending on the payment provider and bank.

After provider dispatch or service start

Once the provider has started travelling to the appointment or the service has started, a refund may be limited depending on the circumstances. We will review genuine service issues, provider no-shows and other exceptional cases fairly and in accordance with applicable law.

Cash bookings

Cash-on-Service bookings are unpaid until the session is completed. If a cash booking is cancelled before service, there is normally no payment to refund.

Provider cancellation

If TherapyOnWay cannot provide the booked service, we will try to offer an alternative time or provider. For a paid booking that cannot be fulfilled, an appropriate refund will be arranged.

Refund requests

For help with a cancellation or refund, contact us with your booking ID, registered mobile number and a short description of the issue. Do not send OTPs, UPI PINs or passwords.

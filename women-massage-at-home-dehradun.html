Comfort and safety

For any home service, choose a comfortable and safe environment. Confirm appointment details before the visit and contact support if you need help. Massage is a wellness service and is not a substitute for medical diagnosis or treatment.

When to seek medical advice

If pain is severe, persistent, unexplained, follows an injury, or comes with concerning symptoms, consult a qualified healthcare professional rather than relying on massage alone.
